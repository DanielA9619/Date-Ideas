ADVENTURES & OUTDOORS
---------------------
[ ] Sunset Picnic
    Cheese board, fruit, wine at a scenic overlook or park.

[ ] Stargazing + Late Night Drive
    Drive somewhere dark, bring blankets & hot chocolate.
    Download a stargazing app and make a playlist for the road.

[ ] Neighborhood Exploration Walk
    Pick a part of town you've never explored. No plan —
    just wander, pop into shops, try a random spot to eat.


FOOD & DINING
-------------
[ ] Cooking Challenge Night
    Pick a cuisine you've never cooked (Thai, Ethiopian, Korean).
    Shop together, cook side by side, rate each other's dishes.

[ ] Farmers Market + Mystery Recipe
    Each person picks 3 surprise ingredients at a farmers market,
    then come home and cook a meal using all of them.

[ ] Dessert Hop
    Skip dinner. Hit 3 spots: a bakery, an ice cream shop,
    and a fancy dessert bar. Rate and compare all night.


ARTS & CULTURE
--------------
[ ] Bookstore & Coffee Date
    Browse a bookstore, each pick a book for the other person,
    then read and sip coffee at a nearby cafe.

[ ] Live Music or Open Mic Night
    Find a local show, jazz bar, or open mic. Bonus points
    if it's a genre you wouldn't normally pick.

[ ] Pottery / Art Class
    Paint-and-sip, pottery studio, or craft workshop.
    Keep what you make as a memento.


COZY / STAY-IN
--------------
[ ] At-Home Spa Night
    Face masks, candles, massage oils, calming playlist.
    Make fancy infused water or mocktails.

[ ] Movie Marathon Night
    Pick a trilogy or director's collection. Build a blanket
    fort, make themed snacks, fully commit to the couch.

[ ] Game Night
    Board games, card games, or video games with a twist —
    loser of each round does a dare or answers a spicy question.


ACTIVE & SPORTY
---------------
[ ] Hike + Hidden Gem Lunch
    Scenic trail in the morning, then reward yourselves
    with lunch at a hole-in-the-wall you've never tried.


UNIQUE / NOVELTY
----------------
[ ] Thrift Store Challenge
    Each person gets $10-15 and 30 min to find the
    funniest outfit for the other. Wear them to dinner.
