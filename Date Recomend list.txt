Since this is our first recommendation list, here's a
diverse mix to help discover what you both enjoy most!

--------------------------------------------------------
  WEEK 1: March 26 - April 1
--------------------------------------------------------

Thu Mar 26 | SUNSET PICNIC
             Pack a simple spread (cheese board, fruit,
             wine or sparkling water) and find a scenic
             overlook or park. Golden hour makes everything
             feel special.
             Vibe: Romantic, low-key

Fri Mar 27 | COOKING CHALLENGE NIGHT
             Pick a cuisine neither of you has cooked
             before (Thai, Ethiopian, Korean). Shop for
             ingredients together, cook side by side, and
             rate each other's dishes.
             Vibe: Fun, collaborative

Sat Mar 28 | FARMERS MARKET + MYSTERY RECIPE
             Hit up a local farmers market in the morning.
             Each person picks 3 surprise ingredients, then
             come home and cook a meal using all of them.
             Vibe: Adventurous, daytime date

Sun Mar 29 | BOOKSTORE & COFFEE DATE
             Browse a bookstore together. Each pick a book
             for the other person, then read and sip coffee
             at a nearby cafe.
             Vibe: Cozy, intellectual

Mon Mar 30 | AT-HOME SPA NIGHT
             Face masks, candles, massage oils, calming
             playlist. Take turns giving each other
             massages. Make fancy infused water or mocktails.
             Vibe: Relaxing, intimate

Tue Mar 31 | NEIGHBORHOOD EXPLORATION WALK
             Pick a part of town you've never wandered.
             Walk with no plan — pop into shops, try a
             random restaurant, photograph cool things
             you find.
             Vibe: Casual, curious

Wed Apr 1  | GAME NIGHT (April Fools Edition)
             Board games, card games, or video games with
             a twist — loser of each round does a dare or
             answers a spicy question.
             Vibe: Playful, competitive

--------------------------------------------------------
  WEEK 2: April 2 - April 8
--------------------------------------------------------

Thu Apr 2  | STARGAZING + LATE NIGHT DRIVE
             Drive somewhere with less light pollution.
             Bring blankets, hot chocolate, and a
             stargazing app. Bonus: make a playlist for
             the drive.
             Vibe: Romantic, adventurous

Fri Apr 3  | LIVE MUSIC OR OPEN MIC NIGHT
             Check local listings for a live show, jazz
             bar, or open mic. Even better if it's a
             genre you wouldn't normally pick.
             Vibe: Energetic, cultural

Sat Apr 4  | HIKE + HIDDEN GEM LUNCH
             Find a scenic trail nearby and hike in the
             morning, then reward yourselves with lunch
             at a hole-in-the-wall restaurant you've
             never tried.
             Vibe: Active, exploratory

Sun Apr 5  | THRIFT STORE CHALLENGE
             Each person gets $10-15. You have 30 minutes
             to find the funniest/best outfit for the
             other person. Wear the outfits to dinner.
             Vibe: Hilarious, cheap date

Mon Apr 6  | MOVIE MARATHON NIGHT
             Pick a trilogy or a director's collection.
             Build a blanket fort, make themed snacks
             to match the movies, and fully commit to
             the couch.
             Vibe: Cozy, low-effort

Tue Apr 7  | POTTERY / ART CLASS
             Look up a local paint-and-sip, pottery
             studio, or craft workshop. Making something
             together is a great bonding experience —
             keep what you make as a memento.
             Vibe: Creative, hands-on

Wed Apr 8  | DESSERT HOP
             Skip dinner — go straight to dessert. Visit
             3 different spots: a bakery, an ice cream
             shop, and a fancy dessert bar. Rate and
             compare all night.
             Vibe: Sweet, spontaneous
