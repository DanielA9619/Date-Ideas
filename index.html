Date Ideas

March 26 – April 8, 2026

Adventures & Outdoors
Sunset Picnic
Cheese board, fruit, wine at a scenic overlook or park. Golden hour makes everything feel special.
Stargazing + Late Night Drive
Drive somewhere dark, bring blankets & hot chocolate. Grab a stargazing app and make a playlist for the road.
Neighborhood Exploration Walk
Pick a part of town you've never explored. No plan — just wander, pop into shops, try a random spot to eat.
Food & Dining
Cooking Challenge Night
Pick a cuisine you've never cooked (Thai, Ethiopian, Korean). Shop together, cook side by side, rate each other's dishes.
Farmers Market + Mystery Recipe
Each person picks 3 surprise ingredients at a farmers market, then come home and cook a meal using all of them.
Dessert Hop
Skip dinner. Hit 3 spots: a bakery, an ice cream shop, and a fancy dessert bar. Rate and compare all night.
Arts & Culture
Bookstore & Coffee Date
Browse a bookstore, each pick a book for the other, then read and sip coffee at a nearby cafe.
Live Music or Open Mic Night
Find a local show, jazz bar, or open mic. Bonus points if it's a genre you wouldn't normally pick.
Pottery / Art Class
Paint-and-sip, pottery studio, or craft workshop. Keep what you make as a memento.
Cozy / Stay-In
At-Home Spa Night
Face masks, candles, massage oils, calming playlist. Make fancy infused water or mocktails.
Movie Marathon Night
Pick a trilogy or director's collection. Build a blanket fort, make themed snacks, fully commit to the couch.
Game Night
Board games, card games, or video games — loser of each round does a dare or answers a spicy question.
Active & Sporty
Hike + Hidden Gem Lunch
Scenic trail in the morning, then reward yourselves with lunch at a hole-in-the-wall you've never tried.
Unique / Novelty
Thrift Store Challenge
Each person gets $10-15 and 30 min to find the funniest outfit for the other. Wear them to dinner.
